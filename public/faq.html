Frequently Asked Questions

Is NetForge free?

Yes. The whole course — lessons, labs, quizzes, and the certificate — is free and will stay free.

Do I need an account?

Yes, to track progress, earn badges, save notes, and take quizzes. Creating an account takes about a minute and only needs a username, email, and password.

Do I need prior networking experience?

No. Start with Networking Basics and work forward. Interactive labs give you hands-on practice without any hardware.

How do quizzes work?

Each lesson ends with a short quiz. You must score 70% or higher to pass and unlock the next lesson. You can retry unlimited times.

What are the labs?

Three built-in tools: a subnet calculator, an OSI layer matcher, and a simulated terminal. Complete the built-in task to mark the lab done.

How do badges and the certificate work?

Badges unlock automatically as you hit milestones. When every lesson is complete, your certificate appears on your certificate page, ready to print.

Where can I report a problem?

See the Report an Issue page for the fastest ways to reach us.
