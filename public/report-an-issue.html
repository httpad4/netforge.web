Report an Issue

Found a bug, a broken link, or a factual error in a lesson? Here is how to let us know.

Before you report

Check the FAQ in case your question is already answered.

How to report

  • Content or technical bug — open a GitHub issue at github.com/netforge/issues.
  • Private details (account, progress) — email support@netforge.example.

What to include

A title that describes the problem, the page or lesson where it happened, what you expected vs. what you saw, and — for technical issues — your browser and device. Screenshots help a lot.
