Trust Center

A plain-language rundown of how NetForge keeps your account and your progress safe.

Security

Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes. Sessions use signed, short-lived tokens in httpOnly, SameSite cookies. All database access uses prepared statements.

Privacy

No trackers, no analytics scripts, no advertising. See the Privacy Policy for the full details.

Data integrity

Progress, badges, and quiz results are computed server-side — they can’t be altered from the browser. See Terms of Use.

Vulnerability reporting

Found a bug that leaks data or lets users cheat? Report it to security@netforge.example. Please don’t post exploit details publicly.
