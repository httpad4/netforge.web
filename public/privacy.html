Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

Account details you provide at sign-up (username, email, optional display name, a hashed password), and the learning data your account generates: lessons completed, quiz attempts and scores, notes, comments, badges, and streaks.

What we don’t do

No third-party trackers, no advertising, no selling or sharing of your personal data. This site runs no analytics scripts.

How it’s stored

Data lives in a SQLite database on our servers. Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes. Sessions use signed, short-lived tokens in an httpOnly cookie.

Your choices

You may request export or deletion of your account data at any time by contacting support. Deleting your account removes your notes, comments, and progress records.
