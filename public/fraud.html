Fraud Alert

NetForge will never ask you for your password outside the sign-in form, never contact you to “verify your account” by sharing codes, and never require payment to continue a course. A curious clause? An email pressuring you to act fast? Chances are it’s a scam.

How to protect yourself

  • Only type credentials on the real netforge domain, over HTTPS.
  • Ignore urgent messages about suspended accounts, refunds, or “limited time offers” from people claiming to be us.
  • Never share your login code, session token, or recovery answers.

If you spot something

Report suspicious emails, pages, or screenshots to support@netforge.example with the subject Fraud.
