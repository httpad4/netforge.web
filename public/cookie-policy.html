Cookie Policy

NetForge keeps cookies to a minimum. Here is exactly what we set and why.

What we use

  • nf_token — a secure, httpOnly login session cookie so you stay signed in across pages. Delete it by logging out.
  • Theme preference — stored in your browser’s localStorage so your light/dark choice sticks. Not a cookie, and never sent to the server.

What we don’t use

No advertising cookies, no third-party trackers, no analytics beacons. Nothing is sold or shared.

Managing cookies

You can clear both items anytime in your browser’s settings. Since nf_token is required for signed-in features, removing it will sign you out.
