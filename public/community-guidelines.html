Community Guidelines

NetForge is a place to learn networking together. Keep it constructive.

Be respectful

Assume good faith. Everyone starts somewhere — no question is too basic, and no answer is too obvious.

Help, don’t spoil

In quizzes and labs, explain how to derive an answer rather than handing it over. Learning is the point.

Stay on topic

Keep discussion about networking and the course. Promotional or off-topic content will be removed.

Report, don’t retaliate

If something violates these guidelines, report it and let moderators handle it.

Consequences

Repeated violations may lead to restricted or removed accounts. We moderate fairly and transparently.
