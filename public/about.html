About NetForge

NetForge — a self-paced network engineering course for students and hobbyists.

The course

NetForge is a self-paced learning platform for computer networking, built for students and hobbyists who want hands-on skills rather than a slideshow. The curriculum spans eight modules — from networking basics and the OSI & TCP/IP models to IP addressing, routing and switching, DNS & DHCP, wireless networking, security, and troubleshooting.

Each module mixes readable lessons with interactive labs (a subnet calculator, an OSI layer matcher, and a simulated terminal), quiz checkpoints that must be passed to progress, and badges that mark milestones along the way. Finish every lesson and a printable certificate is yours.

Why it exists

Most introductory networking content is either a vendor certification roadmap or a dense textbook. NetForge aims for a third path: short, practical lessons you can complete on any device, with instant feedback and visible progress.
