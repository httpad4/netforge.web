Roadmap / Learning Path

A suggested route through the NetForge curriculum. Work in order — later modules assume the earlier ones.

  1. Networking Basics — what networks are, hosts, links, and the vocabulary of the field.
  2. The OSI & TCP/IP Models — layers, encapsulation, and how data travels end to end.
  3. IP Addressing — IPv4/IPv6, subnetting, and CIDR. Try the subnet calculator lab here.
  4. Routing & Switching — forwarding, MAC tables, routing tables, and default gateways.
  5. DNS & DHCP — name resolution and automatic address configuration.
  6. Wireless Networking — Wi-Fi standards, bands, and the 802.11 protocol family.
  7. Security Fundamentals — threats, defense in depth, and common hardening measures.
  8. Troubleshooting — structured problem solving with ping, traceroute, and logs.

Browse all modules or pick up where you left off.
